# Privacy Policy

Asbatty Home Assistant Backup is a personal app used only by its owner.

- The app's only purpose is to let my own Home Assistant server upload and manage its backup files in my own Google Drive account.
- It only accesses files it creates itself in Google Drive.
- No data is collected, sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
- No other people use this app, and no analytics or tracking are used.
- Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
